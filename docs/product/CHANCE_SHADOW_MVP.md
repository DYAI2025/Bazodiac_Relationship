# MVP - Couple Chart Essential

## Value wedge
One complete vertical slice for **Communication and Processing Tempo**.

## Required path
1. Person A and B consent.
2. BFF creates two validated chart snapshots.
3. FuFirE returns released relationship evidence.
4. Mapping produces neutral communication signals.
5. Dynamic engine either emits one dynamic or abstains.
6. Mobile/web show mechanism, Chance, Shadow, evidence, counterevidence and one reflection question.
7. Optional resonance check records `fits`, `partly`, `does_not_fit` or `later` as a separate self-report family.

## MVP product
Essential only: two short profiles, key relationship evidence, up to three released dynamics and reflection. No composite or annual transit dependency.

## Success evidence
- users can accurately explain that Chance and Shadow are two expressions of one mechanism;
- users can locate source evidence within three interactions;
- no user interprets a displayed confidence as relationship success probability in moderated testing;
- abstention and partial results are understood.
