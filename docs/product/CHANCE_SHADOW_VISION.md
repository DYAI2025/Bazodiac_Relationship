# Bazodiac Relationship - Chance & Shadow Vision

Status: recommended architecture baseline (BAZ-64)

## Highest customer value
Bazodiac gives a couple a shared, non-blaming language for recurring dynamics. It does not decide whether the relationship is good, bad, successful or doomed. It externalizes the mechanism between two people, shows the constructive and pressured expressions of the same mechanism, preserves contradictory evidence, and lets the couple compare the hypothesis with lived experience.

Operating formula: **FuFirE calculates. Bazodiac explains. The couple validates.**

## Core mechanism
1. deterministic source calculations;
2. native meaning per tradition;
3. normalized relationship signals;
4. explicit pair interaction rules;
5. neutral relationship dynamic;
6. Chance and Shadow from the same `neutral_mechanism_id`;
7. evidence, counterevidence and limitations;
8. optional resonance check that never changes the astrological calculation.

## Invariants
- no compatibility score;
- no current position on the Chance/Shadow axis;
- no diagnosis, fate or guaranteed outcome;
- no LLM-created facts, rules, weights or evidence;
- insufficient support produces abstention;
- BaZi, Wu Xing, HeHun and Da Yun form one dependent evidence family unless independence is proven.
